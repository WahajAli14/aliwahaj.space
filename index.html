Django API · 2026

Fuel Route Optimizer

Give it any two places in the U.S. and it plans where a truck with a 500-mile range should refuel, how many gallons to buy at each stop, and what the trip will cost.

Stops come from a dynamic program over station and fuel level, so the plan is cost-optimal rather than "fill up at the cheapest pump nearby".

  • One routing-provider call per new route and zero on a cache hit. Start and finish are geocoded locally from Census data.
  • Stations are matched to the route with a KD-tree over 3D unit vectors, so distances stay correct coast to coast.
  • 6,620 of 6,626 stations in the price feed geocoded; the loader reports the other 6.
  • Plans cached 24 hours in Redis, keyed by data version, so a new price file never serves a stale plan.
  • Tests replay saved provider responses and never hit the live API.

RAG backend · in progress

Football Intelligence Platform

A research assistant for football analysts that answers tactical questions from real documents, like the 2025 UEFA Champions League technical report, and shows the passages behind every answer.

The RAG core works today. I treat it as the foundation for a longer build: measured retrieval first, then reranking, guardrails, memory, production hardening, an MCP interface and agents.

  • Three chunking strategies (fixed, paragraph, sentence-semantic) compared on the same queries.
  • Answers return their source passages, ranks and similarity scores; prompts are written to refuse unsupported claims.
  • Token usage and estimated model cost tracked on every request.
